⚡ One-time setup — connect your Google Sheet

  1. Open your Google Sheet and go to File → Share → Publish to web
  2. Under "Link", select the sheet tab and choose CSV format, then click Publish
  3. Copy the published CSV URL and paste it below
  4. The sheet must have these column headers in row 1 (exact spelling):
    Client  Type  Scope  Owner  SignedDate  ExpiryDate  ContractAmt  BilledAmt  Status  Notes

The URL is saved in this browser only — your CFO will need to paste it once on their device too, or you can bake it in (see note at bottom of page).
